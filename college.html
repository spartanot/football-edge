How to read the predictions
Each game shows two win chances. Oddsmakers is the sportsbook line with its margin removed. Data model is our own model built from opponent-adjusted passing and rushing efficiency (PPA, college football's version of EPA), an Elo rating from past scores, and home field. The model is retrained every day on past seasons plus this season's finished games. Use it as a second opinion, not an edge.
Orange marks a model value bet: a moneyline where the model's win chance times the payout beats the cost by at least 3%. The orange score next to a team name (1 to 10) rates the size of the model's edge: about 1 point per 4% of edge, so 3% scores 1 and 40% or more scores 10. It measures how much the model disagrees with the oddsmakers, not how likely the bet is to win.

Team picks fill a starting rating from this season's record. Margin adds 10 Elo per point of average margin; injuries subtract 25 Elo per point. Injury values are rough rules of thumb you choose yourself.

Picks
Each pick uses the betting market's own odds with the sportsbook's margin removed. In every test we ran, that predicted winners more reliably than any model we built, so it's the pick here. Each game also shows the bet with the best expected return across the moneyline, spread and total, using the pick's win chance to price the spread. Almost every bet has a small negative expected return because of the sportsbook's margin, so "best bet" usually means the least costly one. This tab is separate from your play-money balance and bets.
